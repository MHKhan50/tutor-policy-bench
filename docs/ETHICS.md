# Ethics and responsible research

- The default experiment is entirely synthetic and involves no human participants.
- The optional ASSISTments utility uses an existing educational dataset. Raw data are not redistributed by this repository. Follow the dataset provider's terms and cite the exact dataset page.
- The calibration script aggregates by skill and discards student identifiers and free-text answer content. Keep raw student-level data outside version control.
- Do not attempt to re-identify students or combine the data with external identifiers.
- The 2009–2010 dataset is not representative of current learners, all countries, or Pakistan specifically.
- Do not deploy the simulator's policy as an autonomous tutor for children. Real deployment requires educator oversight, age-appropriate safeguards, privacy review, accessibility evaluation, and human-subject research approval.
- Avoid claims of learning efficacy based only on simulated mastery transitions.
